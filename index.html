How Engineers Really Learn

Knowledge does not create engineers. Thinking creates engineers.

The traditional belief is lecture, then knowledge, then examination. IOL works differently — it starts with real work, not a classroom.

01
Real Work
A genuine event happens on the job — a task, a fault, a decision.
02
Reflection (AI sits here, beside you)
The engineer looks back and asks why. AI can offer a question or a related experience — never the answer.
03
Learning
A new understanding forms — inside the engineer, not inside a slide.
04
Capability
The understanding shows up as better judgement in the next piece of real work.
Where AI Fits

AI does not teach. It helps engineers think.

Beside the reflection, not above it: AI suggests a relevant experience, asks a good question, or recommends a resource. It never supplies the answer. The learning still happens inside the engineer.

The A2P Framework

Action to Performance — and to Capability

Performance is no longer the only outcome. Learning is an outcome too.

01
Action
A real task is carried out.
02
Evidence
The outcome is captured, not lost.
03
Analysis
The gap between target and result is examined.
04
Decision
A choice is made about what to do differently.
05
Reflection
Why did it happen this way? What does it mean?
06
Capability
A durable improvement in judgement.
07
Future Performance
Better action, next time round.
We do not design curriculum for work. We design work that develops capability.

Why This Exists

Over the years, I have become convinced that some of the most valuable learning happens while people are doing real work. Yet much of that learning remains invisible.

I hope to contribute to a growing movement that recognises the workplace not only as a place of production, but also as a place of learning.

To support this work, I established PCTeoh Learning Lab. It allows me to remain independent, keep learning, collaborate with organisations, and sustain this effort over the long term.

It is not about building a large company. It is about creating a sustainable way to keep contributing.

If, along the way, this work creates value for organisations, educators, and professionals, it will also create the sustainability I need to continue the journey.
